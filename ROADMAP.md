# 🗺️ Lex-Skill Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the future development of Lex-Skill. As an open-source project, priorities are driven by community needs and legal engineering breakthroughs.

## 🚀 Phase 1: Foundation (Current)
- [x] **Core Framework:** Disclaimer, Risk Framework, and Agentic Verification.
- [x] **Motor vs. Fuel Architecture:** Decoupling legal logic from jurisdiction data.
- [x] **Guardian Validator:** Automated structural and "leakage" testing.
- [x] **Core Skills:** Context Manager, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, NDA, Contract Review, Legal Letter.
- [x] **IDE Integration:** Full support for Cursor, Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Codex, and OpenCode.
